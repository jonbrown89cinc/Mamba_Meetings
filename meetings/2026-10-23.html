Section 04

Make the Math Tangible

Example: 20 transactions today; target 32 next year. That's 12 incremental transactions. At an illustrative 1% lead-to-close conversion, 12 ÷ 0.01 = 1,200 incremental leads annually, or about 100 monthly. At 1–1.25%, plan roughly 80–100 leads per additional closing.

A plan requires lead volume, realistic conversion assumptions and sustained nurturing. Some leads close in later periods.

Section 05

Traditional vs. PRIME

Traditional

The client pays for the platform and funds advertising directly.

For clients able to sustain the ad spend their goal requires.

PRIME

The client invests in the platform; CINC funds lead generation and shares in transaction success via a fee.

For growth-oriented clients seeking lead volume without fully funding ads upfront.

Prescribe, don't present

“Based on your goal, required volume and investment capacity, here's the model I'd recommend—and why.”

Section 06 · PRIME requirement

Transaction Connect + RealSatisfied

Required for PRIME: Connect the client's Transaction Management System (TMS). Optional: Enable RealSatisfied after connecting.

Why the TMS connection matters

Every CINC website includes a Transaction Connect page. The TMS connection provides read-only access to closed-transaction information, helping CINC identify which leads become real business.

We aren't just optimizing for clicks or registrations. We're working toward more closed transactions.

What it helps us improve

Identify leads that become closings, improve Search and Social campaign optimization, find more prospects resembling the client's best customers, and better understand what drives revenue—not just lead volume.

How clients connect

1. Visit the client's CINC website and add /admin/transaction-connect to the domain (example: https://www.URL.COM/admin/transaction-connect).

2. Connect the applicable TMS: SkySlope, dotloop, BoldTrail, zipForm, Lofty or KW Command. Connect multiple systems if applicable.

3. Optional: After connecting the TMS, a prompt appears to enable RealSatisfied. Clients may choose whether to enable it.

Team takeaway: For PRIME, the TMS connection is a requirement. RealSatisfied is not.
