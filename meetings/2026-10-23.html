Section 01

The Details Drive the Difference

Websites, advertising and CRM may sound alike. The details determine business outcomes.

Website conversion

CINC-reported website conversion: 15% versus a 2.88% comparison benchmark (about 5.2×).

More opportunities from the same traffic. Verify comparable definitions.

Hyperlocal lead generation

Target neighborhoods, school districts, country clubs, resorts and points of interest instead of broad-radius targeting.

More relevant leads, less wasted effort and potentially faster closings.

CRM + AI

AI uses website behavior and CRM context to engage leads, recognize intent and alert agents when they become Agent Ready.

Internal example: 13–15 Agent Ready Leads per 100 leads generated.

Section 03

From Platform Salespeople to Lead Generation Consultants

Educate first. Consult second. Recommend third.

Discover

Ask about current and target transactions, lead sources, contact and conversion rates, lead volume, follow-up capacity and goals.

Educate

Explain what lead volume, nurturing and follow-up will be needed to achieve their goal.

Recommend

Prescribe Traditional or PRIME based on their growth plan and investment capacity. Explain why.

Section 04

Make the Math Tangible

Example: 20 transactions today; target 32 next year. That's 12 incremental transactions. At an illustrative 1% lead-to-close conversion, 12 ÷ 0.01 = 1,200 incremental leads annually, or about 100 monthly. At 1–1.25%, plan roughly 80–100 leads per additional closing.

A plan requires lead volume, realistic conversion assumptions and sustained nurturing. Some leads close in later periods.

Section 05

Traditional vs. PRIME

Traditional

The client pays for the platform and funds advertising directly.

For clients able to sustain the ad spend their goal requires.

PRIME

The client invests in the platform; CINC funds lead generation and shares in transaction success via a fee.

For growth-oriented clients seeking lead volume without fully funding ads upfront.

Prescribe, don't present

“Based on your goal, required volume and investment capacity, here's the model I'd recommend—and why.”

Section 06

Friday's Coaching Challenge

Role-play a consultation: an agent closed 20 transactions and wants 32. Ask discovery questions before discussing price. Work backward from 12 incremental closings, explain the lead volume and nurturing needed, then recommend Traditional or PRIME.

“Don't sell clients what they think they need. Help them understand what it takes to achieve what they want.”
