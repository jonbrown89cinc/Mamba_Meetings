Section 01

The Details Drive the Difference

At a high level, lead generation providers can look alike: websites, advertising, CRM. The difference is how the details work together to produce business outcomes.

Website: more opportunities from traffic

CINC-reported website conversion: 15% versus a 2.88% comparison benchmark (approximately 5.2×).

More opportunities from the same website traffic. Validate that benchmarks use comparable definitions.

Hyperlocal advertising: relevance over radius

Target desirable neighborhoods, school districts, clubs and other local points of interest across search and social channels.

More relevant leads, potentially less wasted effort and shorter paths to a transaction.

CRM + AI: engagement into appointments

CINC AI monitors website activity and CRM context, converses with consumers, and notifies agents when a lead becomes Agent Ready.

Less manual chasing and more timely conversations. Internal example: 13–15 Agent Ready Leads per 100 generated leads.

Section 02

The Small Details That Compound

Teach reps to connect every capability to the agent's day-to-day business.

Behavior-based property alerts

Listing alerts adapt as a consumer's search behavior changes over time.

The buyer continues seeing relevant homes without the agent manually rebuilding searches.

Intelligent email timing

Property alerts are timed around a consumer's observed email engagement habits.

A better chance the message is seen when the consumer is ready to engage.

Agent Ready notifications

AI identifies signs that a consumer wants to speak, tour, buy or sell and alerts the agent.

Agents can prioritize meaningful opportunities rather than treating every lead equally.

Section 03

From Platform Salespeople to Consultants

Educate first. Consult second. Recommend third.

Discover

Learn current transactions, target transactions, lead sources, lead volume, conversion, contact rates and follow-up capacity.

What is the client trying to accomplish?

Educate

Explain the lead-volume math, long nurturing cycles and what technology needs to do to support conversion.

Help clients understand what success requires.

Build the strategy

Work backward from incremental closings to approximate lead volume, budget, nurturing and staffing.

Define a realistic plan, not just a package.

Recommend

Prescribe Traditional or PRIME based on goals, investment capacity and economics; explain why.

Don't just present options. Prescribe solutions.

Section 04

Make the Math Tangible

Example: an agent closed 20 transactions and wants 32 next year.

12 additional closings

32 − 20 = 12 incremental transactions.

One incremental closing per month, on average.

At a 1% lead-to-close assumption

12 ÷ 0.01 = 1,200 incremental leads annually.

Approximately 100 additional leads per month.

Planning range

At 1%–1.25% conversion, 80–100 leads per incremental closing.

Actual results vary; leads generated this year may close in a later period.

Section 05

Traditional vs. PRIME

The right model follows discovery—not the other way around.

Traditional

The client pays for the CINC platform and funds their own advertising.

May suit clients who can sustain the advertising spend needed for their goals.

PRIME

The client invests in the platform; CINC funds lead generation and shares in transaction success through a success fee.

May suit growth-oriented clients who need higher lead volume without fully funding advertising upfront.

Recommendation talk track

“Based on your transaction goal, the volume we'll need, and your investment capacity, here's the model I'd recommend—and why.”

Show the alternative, but lead with a reasoned recommendation.

Section 06

Friday's Coaching Challenge

Practice a consultation—not a feature dump.

Role-play scenario

Agent completed 20 transactions in 2026 and wants 32 in 2027.

Ask discovery questions before mentioning pricing.

Prove the strategy

Explain ~1,200 incremental annual leads at a 1% planning conversion, plus follow-up and nurturing requirements.

Recommend Traditional or PRIME and explain the tradeoffs.

Team takeaway

“Don't sell clients what they think they need. Help them understand what it takes to achieve what they want.”

Every prospect should leave knowing more about how to grow their business.
